# 买量视频音乐提示词

## 方案一：快节奏电子（推荐买量用）

> Genre: lo-fi electronic battle music, chillstep, synthwave
> Instruments: warm 808 kick drum, tight punchy snare, smooth bass synth, soft analog pads, gentle arpeggios
> Mood: energetic but smooth, cool, catchy, addictive
> Structure: 25 seconds, fast drum pattern throughout, no harsh elements
> BPM: 145
> Keywords: snow theme, pixel game soundtrack, head-bobbing beat, earworm melody, warm mixing, low-pass filtered
> Avoid: dubstep, orchestral, cinematic, high-pitched, piercing, screeching, metallic sounds, aggressive

**中文说明：** Lo-fi 电子战斗音乐/Chillstep风格。暖调混音，鼓点节奏保留但声音不尖锐。用低通滤波让高频不刺耳，保持节奏感的同时听起来"暖"。

## 方案二：硬核摇滚（备选）

> Genre: high-energy punk rock instrumental
> Instruments: distorted electric guitar riff, fast punk drum beat (double kick), bass slap
> Mood: aggressive, fun, ridiculous, over-the-top
> Structure: 25 seconds, no intro, straight into the riff
> BPM: 160
> Keywords: cartoon battle music, skull shooting zombies, ice world, arcade game
> Avoid: orchestral, slow sections, complex arrangements

**中文说明：** 硬核朋克摇滚，直接上riff，不要前奏。失真吉他+双踩鼓点，卡通战斗感。适合搭配快速击杀怪物的画面剪辑。
